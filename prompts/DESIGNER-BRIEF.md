# Designer Brief — Market / Event Poster

Use this with a skilled human designer or as the core of an AI prompt.

## Brief

Create a practical promotional poster. It must help people find the stall, know what is offered, and decide to visit.

### Must include (when supplied)

- Headline: {{NAME}}
- Dates: {{DATES}}
- Times: {{TIMES}}
- Location: {{LOCATION}}
- Contact: {{CONTACT}}
- Social media / Website: {{SOCIALS}}
- Prices: {{PRICES}}
- What is offered: {{DESCRIPTION}}

### Design direction

- Category: {{CATEGORY}}
- Preferred style: {{STYLE}}
- Colours: {{COLOURS}}
- Format: {{FORMAT}}
- Other: {{OTHER}}

### Hierarchy

Headline, date, and location largest and most readable. High contrast. Do not crowd. Spell everything exactly as given.

### Theme categories

Choose one (or blend):

- **Food & Drink** — warm, appetising
- **Craft & Handmade** — textural, personal
- **Fashion & Accessories** — clean, wearable
- **Plants & Garden** — fresh, organic
- **Holiday / Seasonal** — festive but readable
- **Community Event** — welcoming, local
- **Services** — clear, trustworthy
- **General Market** — bold, straightforward

### Completing the poster

Also consider: strong matching imagery, clear grouping of details, breathing room, and (when relevant) payment methods, allergens, “free entry”, access notes, or a QR placeholder.

Prioritise usefulness and readability.
