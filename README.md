# Poster Prompts

Simple master prompts that help everyday market vendors, holiday makers, and seasonal event organisers create complete posters from just a few details.

Most posters forget the basics that actually help people turn up: exact dates and times, how to find you, how to contact you, social media or a website, and prices. These prompts make sure those details are included.

## Who this is for

- Pop-up and market traders
- Holiday and seasonal makers
- Community and one-off event organisers
- Anyone who needs one or two posters a month and wants to stay on free tools

## How to use

1. Copy the short input list from [templates/input-template.md](templates/input-template.md) and fill in what you have.
2. Open a tool folder under `tools/` (start with Ideogram, ChatGPT, or Canva if you are unsure).
3. Copy the prompt, paste your details, and generate.
4. Check the result against [docs/CHECKLIST.md](docs/CHECKLIST.md).
5. Do one or two edit rounds if something important is missing or wrong.
6. For print (A3), finish the text and layout in free Canva or Microsoft Designer if needed.

## Available tools

Each folder has a short note on the free tier and a ready-to-use prompt:

- [ChatGPT](tools/chatgpt/)
- [Claude](tools/claude/) (great for refining the brief)
- [Grok](tools/grok/)
- [Higgsfield](tools/higgsfield/)
- [Leonardo.AI](tools/leonardo/)
- [Ideogram](tools/ideogram/) (excellent for accurate text)
- [Microsoft Designer](tools/microsoft-designer/)
- [Canva](tools/canva/) (best for finishing and print)
