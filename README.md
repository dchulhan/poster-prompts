# Poster Prompts

Simple prompts and briefs that help everyday market vendors, holiday makers, and seasonal event organisers create complete posters from just a few details.

Most posters forget the basics that actually help people turn up: exact dates and times, how to find you, how to contact you, social media or a website, and prices. These prompts make sure those details are included.

## Who this is for

- Pop-up and market traders
- Holiday and seasonal makers
- Community and one-off event organisers
- Anyone who needs one or two posters a month and wants to stay on free tools
- Skilled human designers who want a clear, complete brief

## How to use

1. Copy the short input list from [templates/input-template.md](templates/input-template.md) and fill in what you have (including a Category).
2. For a human designer: use the [Designer Brief](prompts/DESIGNER-BRIEF.md).
3. For AI: open a tool folder under `tools/` (start with Ideogram, ChatGPT, or Canva if unsure), copy the prompt, and paste your details.
4. Check the result against [docs/CHECKLIST.md](docs/CHECKLIST.md).
5. Do one or two edit rounds if something important is missing or wrong.
6. For print (A3), finish the text and layout in free Canva or Microsoft Designer if needed.

## Theme categories

The poster can vary by theme. See [docs/CATEGORIES.md](docs/CATEGORIES.md) and set the Category field:

Food & Drink · Craft & Handmade · Fashion & Accessories · Plants & Garden · Holiday / Seasonal · Community Event · Services · General Market
